QUICK FIXES

Troubleshooting, step by step.

Start with the basics

Confirm your internet is working, check your subscription is active, and make sure your username and password match exactly. Login details are case sensitive.

01Cloud error / 451A channel won’t play

In the WKTV app, this message may appear when your subscription has expired or when a live-event channel is no longer active. The message alone does not confirm the cause.

  1. Switch to a different regular live channel.
  2. Check your subscription renewal date.
  3. If your plan has expired, contact WKTV to renew.
  4. If an active account still cannot play other channels, contact support.
Get help or renew ↗
02No channelsYour playlist is empty or out of date

If the app hasn’t been opened for a few days, the playlist may need a refresh. Confirm your subscription is active first.

  1. Open WKTV and select Settings in the bottom-left corner.
  2. Select Playlist.
  3. Tap Refresh Playlist and allow the update to finish.
  4. Return to Live TV and try a channel.
03EPG / TV guide missingRefresh the program schedule
  1. Open WKTV Settings.
  2. Select EPG.
  3. Tap Update and allow the guide to finish loading.
Some custom channels do not have EPG.

Custom channels labeled “Hulu” or “HBO,” for example, may not display program listings. Missing guide data on those channels does not necessarily mean the stream is broken.

04Freezing or bufferingCheck your Wi-Fi connection
  1. Make sure the streaming device is connected to the strongest stable Wi-Fi network available.
  2. Move closer to your router, or use Ethernet if your device supports it.
  3. Pause large downloads or other heavy internet use, then test again.
  4. Restart your streaming device and router.
  5. Try a different channel. If only one channel fails, tell support which one.

Also check your plan’s connection limit. Streaming on more devices than your plan allows can interrupt playback.

05Clear cache / dataLast step if problems continue

On Android / Fire TV devices, menu names may vary. Try clearing cache first; only clear data if the issue continues.

  1. Open your device’s Settings.
  2. Select Applications / Apps and find WKTV.
  3. Select Clear Cache, reopen WKTV, and test again.
  4. If needed, return to the same menu and select Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  5. Open WKTV and sign back in.
Have your login details ready.

Clearing data removes saved app settings and signs you out. Make sure you have your server details, username, and password before continuing.
