A FEW GOOD ANSWERS

Before you press play.

Can I install WKTV on more than one device?

You can set up compatible devices, but your plan determines how many can stream simultaneously. One connection supports one active stream; five connections support five.

How does the free trial work?

The free trial lasts 24 hours and includes 2 connections, not just one, so you can share it with a friend or family member. Use it to check the lineup and performance on your own devices and internet connection. No credit card is required.

What if I need help setting up?

Follow the step-by-step setup guide for your device, join our Facebook support group, or contact WKTV Support for help with device compatibility, login, playlists, or an existing subscription.

Are lifetime plans available?

Yes. Lifetime access is a one-time payment of $600 for one connection or $850 for five connections. Get lifetime · 1 connection or lifetime · 5 connections.

Can I buy a streaming box with WKTV already set up?

Yes. Choose an ONN 4K Plus ($100 one-time setup & activation) or ONN 4K Pro ($150 one-time setup & activation), then add a plan: monthly at $30 for 1 connection, or annual with 1 to 5 connections. WKTV comes pre-installed and we ship it to you. See streaming boxes.

Can I try it for a full month?

Yes. The one-month trial is $20 for one connection. Start the one-month trial.
